# Community code of conduct

Jidan welcomes people of every language, background, identity, and experience level.

- Be kind, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's device data or credentials.
- Clearly label experiments that can change device or external state.
- Give translators room to improve wording without treating one dialect as the only valid form.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations. Serious or private concerns can be reported through the repository's private security-reporting channel.
